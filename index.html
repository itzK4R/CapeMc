Skin Art Maker

Choose an image to begin.

How to use it
  1. Choose an image. It's resized to 72×24 pixels.
  2. Download the zip and unzip it. It holds skin-01.png to skin-26.png.
  3. Apply skin-26 first, then 25, 24 and so on down to skin-01, and finish with your normal skin.
  4. After each one, open your NameMC profile and wait until that skin shows in your history before applying the next.

Mojang limits how fast you can change skins. If a change fails, wait a few minutes and try again.

About CapeMC

Every cape has a story.

What Is CapeMC?

CapeMC is a community-made project dedicated to documenting Minecraft capes in one organized place. Discover official capes, explore their designs, learn about their history, and find out how they were distributed.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve Minecraft cape history and make information about every cape easier to find and understand. We aim to create a reliable and accessible archive for everyone interested in Minecraft capes.
